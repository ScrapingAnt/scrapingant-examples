# Prospective Apify continuation methods

These are closed, secret-free offline methods and explicitly synthetic fixtures.
They contain no measured customer result, invoice, completed-run evidence or
claim that a provider is cheapest. Publication and every paid dispatch require
the exact reviewed scope to be approved. `ACTIVE_CELL = None` is the shipped state.

The seven planned identities preserve six previously unstarted assignments and
add one conditional Contact Details pilot. AI and Google are prospective contract
changes, not retroactive repairs to their first-run outcomes. The original AI
object-only score remains separately reported. Its new strict normalizer permits
one exact five-field object or a singleton array containing that object, without
coercion, truncation, choosing among multiple objects or rewriting captured bytes.
This is a tested normalization contract, not a documented universal Actor shape.

Google permits exactly three default stores and one registered `linkProspecting`
dataset. The extra must have a distinct ID, match the new run, Actor and owner,
have no name, be created within the run/observation window, and have integer zero
`itemCount` and `stats.storageBytes` at both bounded metadata captures. No extra
dataset content is fetched. Aliases must match at start, every poll and the final
meter. This deliberately narrow pilot fails if the required alias is absent or
changes. Two empty observations establish neither continuous emptiness nor the
absence of every SDK access to named or prior storage. The prior policy failure
remains unchanged. Useful output is three assigned query pages, with Google query
URL, page identity and at least one expected-domain organic result; organic-result
counts are diagnostic and never replace the three-query denominator.

WCC uses the exact original inputs, build and 30 assignments for each remaining
cell. It retains the original primary text rule: at least 95% of distinct sentence
IDs and exact whitespace-normalized sentences, at most one distinct boilerplate
ID, and native SUCCEEDED. Markdown and formatting markers are diagnostic. No
`ignoreCanonicalUrl` counterfactual is inserted. The original 819-observation
design and its prior outcomes remain intact.

Contact Details has six flat, owned pages with fictional NANP 555-0100–0105 numbers
and reserved example.com email addresses. No page contains outbound links,
JavaScript or frames. Depth zero, six maximum requests, one per start URL, no
merging, no browser, no residential proxy, and disabled enrichment/social/email
verification are explicit native inputs. Acceptance requires each exact page and
start URL, exact expected email and confident-phone sets, no false positives or
unexpected enrichment. Uncertain phones remain diagnostic. Missing or duplicate
pages and native error rows remain in the assigned denominator. The documented
Linktree exception cannot be triggered by this corpus. Internal request retries
and complete wire/storage-access absence are not proven by these inputs.

E-commerce's six Product JSON-LD pages and offline Offer metrics are synthetic
prerequisites only. That Actor is excluded from the producer and dispatch choices.
Its documented minimum cap is $1; setting `disableFallbacks` still permits
marketplace standby, its result limit does not establish a finite request bound,
and its separate `errors` dataset requires an additional reviewed capture policy.
SKU is an optional diagnostic; one exact Offer price/currency and URL/name are
the modeled output contract. Smart Article Extractor is also excluded pending
verifiable source/dependency and independent storage-access evidence.

Each reviewed start is pinned to its build, LIMITED_PERMISSIONS, memory, timeout,
maximum charge and `restartOnError=false`. The transport attempts one start, no
redirect/retry or arbitrary/account/storage-list route, at most seven polls, one
raw export with all native error/hidden/empty rows and one sentinel, three default
metadata GETs, one log and one final meter. Google adds precisely two metadata-only
GETs. A registered-storage breach while the new run is active may attempt one
abort of that same bound run; it authorizes no old-run operation. Total attempted
calls are bounded by 17, total transport time by 600 seconds, workflow by 15 minutes.
HTTP/body/deadline failures stop. Captures are authenticated-encrypted for the
existing recipient; public artifacts reveal only fixed state/count/hash fields.
No deletion or cleanup is authorized. The default-store item count is checked
after one six-second settle. Run caps and ancillary reserves are planning holds,
not minimum spend, invoices or guaranteed maximum eventual all-in charges.

Before any dispatch, the private owner must bind direct exact-scope approval,
publication manifest, exact-source native closed CI on Python 3.10 and 3.12,
served fixture hashes, stopped prior workers, all other closed guards, an
authenticated fresh two-GET account preflight, a retained budget within $19/72,
and a durable exclusive consumed-attempt intent. Every cell is sequential and
new R3 AI/Google needs a successful authenticated new R2 with complete assigned
useful output and storage policy. A failed/ambiguous start stays consumed; do not
retry it, replay a consumed billing workflow or infer a zero charge. After each
cell, close the guard and reconcile its evidence before considering the next.
Do not use the historical offline AI normalization result to admit R3.

The private preflight uses only the already reviewed monthly-usage and limits
GET paths, with a fresh one-time intent/approval; this namespace adds no account
routes. Public-source activation and paid execution are subsequent operations.
